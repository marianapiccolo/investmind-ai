# Investment Analysis Limitations

Investment analytics summarize historical data and model assumptions.

They can support understanding and comparison, but they do not eliminate
uncertainty and should not be interpreted as guarantees of future outcomes.

## Historical Data Limitations

Historical metrics depend on the observations included in the analysis.

Changing the start date, end date, data frequency, or available market history
can materially change calculated performance and risk metrics.

Historical relationships may also change over time.

## Data Quality

Analytical results depend on the quality and completeness of the underlying
data.

Missing prices, stale market data, incorrect transactions, incomplete cash
flows, or inconsistent currencies can affect portfolio calculations.

When required data is unavailable, the limitation should be disclosed rather
than replaced with invented values.

## Model and Metric Limitations

Every financial metric simplifies reality.

Volatility does not measure every form of risk.

Maximum drawdown depends on the historical period observed.

Correlation measures historical co-movement and can change during market
stress.

Risk-adjusted metrics depend on their definitions and assumptions.

No single metric should therefore be interpreted as a complete description of
a portfolio.

## Benchmark Limitations

Benchmark comparisons are meaningful only when the selected benchmark is
relevant to the portfolio being evaluated.

A portfolio containing different asset classes, currencies, regions, or risk
exposures may not be directly comparable with a single benchmark.

Benchmark performance should provide context rather than automatically define
whether a portfolio is appropriate.

## Forward-Looking Uncertainty

Historical analytics cannot reliably predict future market prices or returns.

Markets respond to changing economic conditions, interest rates, company
fundamentals, investor behavior, geopolitical events, and many other factors.

Future outcomes may therefore differ substantially from historical patterns.

## Educational Scope

InvestMind AI is designed to explain portfolio analytics and financial
concepts.

Its analytical outputs should be treated as educational information rather
than personalized investment instructions.

Portfolio decisions depend on individual objectives, financial circumstances,
risk tolerance, liquidity needs, tax considerations, and investment horizon.
